Toys Mystery
A charming 3D puzzle game where you help a child navigate a mysterious, shifting house to find their hidden toys.

Developed by Masab Farooque using Unity 2021.3.21

Overview

In "Toys Mystery," you play as a young child whose favorite toys keep disappearing, seemingly taken by a strange, blue figure. Each level presents a multi-room environment where the child must explore, interact with objects, and find the hidden toy. The challenge lies in discovering the correct path through the house as the rooms may dynamically shift and rearrange.
Features
Engaging 3D Graphics: Experience a colorful and stylized 3D world designed with a cartoon aesthetic.
Dynamic Puzzle Gameplay: Navigate levels where the house structure changes as you progress, requiring you to find the right path.
Multiple Levels: Play through several levels, each presenting a new layout and challenge.
Collect Lost Toys: Recover different toys hidden by the mysterious figure in each level.
Simple Controls: Easy-to-learn touch controls for movement and interaction.

Gameplay

The goal of each level is to find the specific toy that has been hidden.
Explore: Guide the child character through the current room.
Find the Path: The house is a mystery! Look for clues or observe how the rooms might rearrange to reveal a new path. The magnifying glass icon may provide an overview of the level structure.
Retrieve the Toy: Once you reach the room where the toy is hidden, guide the child to pick it up.
Level Complete: Finding the toy and bringing it back to bed completes the level
The mysterious figure may appear to hide the toy or potentially act as an obstacle, although the primary challenge is the navigational puzzle.
